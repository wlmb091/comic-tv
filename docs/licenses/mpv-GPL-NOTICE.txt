mpv (video player engine)

This application includes libmpv and its libraries from libmpv-android 1.0.0
(dev.jdtech.mpv:libmpv:1.0.0, https://github.com/jarnedemeulemeester/libmpv-android,
tag v1.0.0, commit fcf6745703dc1265bca88f12fee8fc355ddf251e).

Files: libmpv.so, libplayer.so, libavcodec.so, libavformat.so, libavfilter.so,
libavdevice.so, libavutil.so, libswresample.so, libswscale.so and libc++_shared.so.
